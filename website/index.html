The tour

Five places you'll spend your time

  1. 01

    Home

    Continue watching sits at the top, For You right below it, and the featured title changes as you browse.

  2. 02

    Title pages

    Episodes, downloads and ratings together, and the Watch button takes the title's own colour.

  3. 03

    The player

    Skip the opening and ending, pick audio and subtitles, and a backup player takes over if one can't play.

  4. 04

    Manga

    Continue Reading keeps your place in every series, and For You suggests what to read next.

  5. 05

    Manga title pages

    Scores from AniList, MyAnimeList, MangaUpdates and MangaDex side by side, chapters one click away.

Features

Everything in one place

From the next episode to the next chapter, without juggling tabs or dodging ads.

  1. 01

    A player made for anime

    Skip opening and ending, sub or dub, seek previews, and the next episode lined up.

  2. 02

    Watch offline

    Download one episode or a whole season, within a storage limit you set.

  3. 03

    Read manga

    A fullscreen reader that remembers your page, with chapters grouped by volume.

  4. 04

    Movies and shows

    Trending films and series, season grids and your own watch list.

  5. 05

    For You

    Recommendations worked out on your computer. No account needed.

  6. 06

    Never miss an episode

    An airing schedule, new episodes and chapters ready for you, and an Up Next queue.

  7. 07

    Your lists, your choice

    Track locally, or sync with AniList and Simkl. Offline edits sync later.

  8. 08

    Find anything fast

    One search for anime, manga, movies and TV, with filters and typo fixes.

Privacy

No ads, not even inside the players. No telemetry. Nothing sold or shared. Your data stays on your computer.
