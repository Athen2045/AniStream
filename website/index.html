Features

Everything in one place

From the next episode to the next chapter, without juggling tabs or dodging ads.

A player made for anime

Skip opening and ending, sub or dub, seek previews, and the next episode lined up.

Watch offline

Download one episode or a whole season, within a storage limit you set.

Read manga

A fullscreen reader that remembers your page, with chapters grouped by volume.

Movies and shows

Trending films and series, season grids and your own watch list.

For You

Recommendations worked out on your computer. No account needed.

Never miss an episode

An airing schedule, new episodes and chapters ready for you, and an Up Next queue.

Your lists, your choice

Track locally, or sync with AniList and Simkl. Offline edits sync later.

Find anything fast

One search for anime, manga, movies and TV, with filters and typo fixes.

Title pages

All of a show on one page

Episodes, downloads and ratings together, with a button that takes you straight to the next episode.

Player

A player made for anime

Skip the opening and ending, choose audio and subtitles, and if one player can't play an episode, a backup takes over by itself.

Manga

Pick up the next chapter

Continue Reading keeps your place across every series, and For You suggests what to read next.

Manga title pages

Scores from every source

AniList, MyAnimeList, MangaUpdates and MangaDex ratings side by side, with chapters one click away.

Privacy

Your data stays on your computer

  • No ads. Not in the app, and the players it opens run without their ad scripts.
  • No telemetry. AniStream doesn't report what you watch or read to anyone.
  • Yours to keep. Progress lives in a local database you can back up, and every account link is optional.
